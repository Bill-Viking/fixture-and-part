The brain stores the fixture, not the parts; each event rewrote the model a little and was then mostly let go, and the forgetting is what makes the gist usable. The nearest human case to a perfect ledger is in section 06, and the people who have it describe a feed rather than a life. <EVIDENCE · hsam> So the hard part of that road is not keeping track of more. It is learning what to let go, and letting the events that mattered move the weights rather than fill the notes. An orchestrator that does the first is a very good clerk.
