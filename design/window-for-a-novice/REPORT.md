## 1. What was built
- `src/lib/onnxScan.js`: `columnStatsOf(bytes, manifest, name)` — the one implementation of the column numbers (mean and population sd of every column over all rows, one pass, Float64); `standoutColumns(stats)` — the pure rule: |mean − mean of means| > 4 × sd of means; "same" when sd < median sd / 20, else "high"/"low" by the sign of the mean.
- `scripts/read-model-file.mjs`: reads `tokenizer.json` (sha checked, `--tokenizer <path>` or download); writes top-level `columnStats[wte] = {rows, cols, mean[768], sd[768]}` (4 decimals) and `rowLabels[wte]` (24 pieces, `Ġ` → `␣`); adds `provenance.tokenizer = {url, sha256, bytes}`.
- `src/content/fileFacts.json` regenerated (table below).
- `src/lib/modelBytesWorker.js`, `src/lib/fileBytes.js`: `file-column-stats` message and `readColumnStats(name)`.
- `src/instruments/FileView.jsx`: everything new painted on the one canvas (no new DOM in the box): row labels, slot labels with the two standout marks (▲), the key (same `rampColour`, same square-root law, ticks 0 · 0.05 · 0.2 · 0.5 · maxAbs). Gutter sizes read from CSS custom properties; clicks map through the gutters, a gutter click picks nothing; hover is a ref plus a repaint. Eyebrow, two-span readout and aria-label per WORDS. Live cross-checks: 768 + 768 column numbers at 1e-4 and the 24 row labels against `tokenText`, both through `noteMismatch`.
- `src/styles.css`: box 252 → 294 px at ≥ 640 (top band 20, bottom band 22, left band 38; cell height 10.417 unchanged, cell width 10.56 → 9.97 because the left band comes out of the width); at ≤ 640 box 126 → 140 (key band only — a 5.2 px row cannot carry a label). Readout and eyebrow heights set from the measurements in gauntlet 7.
- Colours: labels `--frozen-on-screen` (the eyebrow's `--frozen` is not allowed as text on a dark panel under the palette law); lit = `--frozen-lit`. No new colour, font or transition.
- Docs: `README.md` and `interactive-guide-spec.md` describe the labels, the key and the standout rule; the spec's 10 px text floor for instrument E names its one exception (the canvas labels are 9 px).
