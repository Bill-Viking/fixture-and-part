## What the specimen shows (the session's reading, not Bill's words)

1. **The render stood.** Ten seconds of staring did not correct it. This is section 08's own claim lived once: what you see is the render, and the eye only supplies the difference. While he stared, no difference got through. The moth was the render and the render was what he saw.
2. **The gap corrected it.** He left, came back, and the next look was a fresh pass. It got as far as "wait..." and then snapped. Two things in that: the correction got in BETWEEN looks, not inside one; and it was a flip, not a drift. The picture did not slowly become a hanger. It snapped whole.
3. **The second pass was not the first pass rerun.** He came back with a vacuum in his hand, from a different distance, maybe a different angle. The state on the far side of the gap was different. That is the honest asymmetry the seam note already carries: the human crosses the seam changed.
4. **Dreams.** His sentence adds replay to the essay's "dreams are the clincher": the renderer running stored patterns through new situations with no input at all. The essay's line is the renderer with the retina silent; his is the renderer at play. Same organ, same trick.
