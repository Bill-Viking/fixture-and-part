## Now
"The Fixture and the Part" — an engineering-drawing-styled interactive guide to transformer internals (React/Vite), built from a static essay with six live instruments (the file, tokenizer, the forward pass drawn live, forward-pass/KV stepper, attention inspector, glass pass). Phase 1 is complete and working: every number is an illustrative heuristic (`src/lib/toyModel.js`), clearly labelled as such, no ML dependency. Phase 2 (real `distilgpt2` via transformers.js) and Phase 3A (the glass pass: per-layer residual stream + logit lens, Instrument D) are verified, merged and deployed, as is Instrument E "the file" (section 01: the real ONNX file — manifest, byte bar, raw-byte blob, byte-exact bell curve, sha-checked re-read; shipped precompute so it shows real facts before any download). The light-theme pass is in progress on the `paper` branch: the page moves off the dark palette onto a white page ground ("Gallery white" — near-white ground, near-black ink, the three role colours at full strength, chosen 2026-08-22 after a cream version was rejected as too beige), dense data fields go onto dark screens set into it, the design tokens are renamed for their colour-law roles, and the copy is rewritten in plain words — including the two moments a reader got stuck on, which are now answered on the page (which sentence every instrument is reading, and what happens to the file when the text changes). Arc 2b shipped 2026-08-23: a decoding control in instrument B (greedy or a seeded sampled draw, sampled by default, because greedy on a six-block model loops within a few tokens — the greedy path itself is unchanged and verified byte-identical to main), and Instrument F "the forward pass, live" — the whole of distilgpt2 drawn once in section 02, its shapes and byte counts read out of the file's own manifest, with the reader's sentence running down it as one amber column per token, a node at each of the seven residual stops sized by the real ‖residual‖ there, attention threads and twelve lit head squares in the selected block, and lettered windows onto A–E. This is what Bill asked for when he said "the neural net is static — I thought we'd see the weight activations as we ran it". Arc 3 "the waterfall" also shipped 2026-08-23: the metaphor is that the file is cut rock training carved and inference never touches, and the activations are water falling through it. Every steel box now wears its own tensor as a faint 48×12 whole-tensor thumbnail read out of the real file at build time — real in both modes — which glints amber as the water crosses it; the selected token's residual falls down the drawing as a 768-cell amber strip, one cell per number, morphing at each of the seven stops to that stop's real values and parking wherever a node is clicked; the columns are finally named on the drawing itself; the logit lens whispers what it hears at each depth as the water passes it; and the last stream lands at the unembedding as a splash of the real next-word probabilities with the sampler's actual pick marked. Next: Arc 4 "melding streams" — Bill's verdict on shipped F is that the engineering drawing won the fight with the waterfall, and his reference image sets the new direction: one continuous fall in a tall dark canyon, attention as strands that meld between streams mid-fall, the machinery receding into the canyon walls as faint etchings from the real tensor bytes.
