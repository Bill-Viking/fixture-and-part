## Important
- Colour law is the visual thesis, not decoration: frozen = machinery (weights/dies), moving = the moving part (activations/tokens), keys = searchable metadata — don't swap this mapping. The tokens are named for those roles, so the colours filling them can be repainted (they have been, once) without touching the law.
- Zero layout shift is a standing invariant, and it is measured rather than assumed — CLS via a buffered PerformanceObserver, plus a geometry diff before and after the model load, because a shift below the fold does not register as CLS. The diff is over the page's structural boxes — the header, every section, every instrument slot and the foot — and not over every element in the document, so it catches a block that moves and would not catch a word that shifts inside one. Two shifts are known and accepted rather than fixed:
  - Instrument A's token strip reflows when the real model lands and the word-split pills are replaced by BPE pieces (~0.058 at 1280, and only while instrument A is on screen). The pills are genuinely different words of genuinely different widths; the strip's own height is already reserved, so nothing outside it moves. Inherent, pre-existing, and measured identically on the palette before this one.
  - The cache counter in instrument B reports ~2e-6 on the STEP that takes the entry count from one digit to two. The number's box is reserved and does not move; the digits inside it do.
  - Typing measures a hairline — 3.1e-5 at 1280, 6.8e-5 at 390 — from one text node re-wrapping in the prose that quotes the reader's sentence. Measured identically on main to the last digit, so it predates instrument F and the waterfall; it is a sub-pixel reflow of a curly quote, not anything moving.
  - Instrument F adds no third one, before the repaint or after it. On `memory-room`, model load, RUN THE PASS, a token click either way, BLOCK and HEAD chips, a wall click, OPEN IN THE FILE, STEP×2, the decoding toggle and the mode toggle all measure 0.000000 at both 1280 and 390, and the structural-box geometry diff across the model load moves nothing inside instrument F at either width (every mover is another instrument's own content: A's pills, B's candidate rows, C's query tokens, D's lens bars). The observer is proved rather than trusted — a 40 px block inserted into the instrument body in view reads 0.0128 at 1280 and 0.0252 at 390. Two shifts the old drawing had were fixed rather than accepted — a control swapping for a ghost line and moving the button beside it, and a right-aligned status note whose glyphs walked sideways whenever the wording changed length. Both are the same failure and have the same fix: pin the box, align the text left.
- The height of instrument F's drawing is a function of its breakpoint and of nothing else — not of how many tokens are in the sequence, not of which register is open, not of whether a model has loaded. Measured: the viewBox is 1166 × 2926.725 for an empty box, one token, three tokens, eight and twenty-three. Two things pay for that. The legend prints live numbers, so each of its four entries has a reserved line count and clips rather than reflows (measured usage against the reservation: 8/9, 9/10, 12/13, 7/8 at 1280 and 7/8, 8/9, 13/14, 6/7 at 390 across illustrative, real, a long sentence and a two-token one). And the number of filaments a stream is drawn with steps with the token count — 64 × 12 dimensions up to twelve tokens, 32 × 24 to twenty-four, 16 × 48 beyond — which is stated on the drawing and also holds the element count roughly flat.
- Phase 1's numbers are explicitly heuristic, not real model output — keep that labelling intact if Phase 2 stays unmerged, so the two modes are never confused.
- Text drawn over a texture cannot be checked from the stylesheet. Instrument F's steel boxes carry a tensor thumbnail under their words, so the ground beneath a glyph is whatever that tensor's bytes happen to be there; contrast has to be measured off the raster (render twice, once with the text hidden, and score every glyph pixel against the pixel underneath it). Doing that found what a stylesheet sweep could not: the selected box's name line had dropped to 4.41:1, below AA across 99.4% of the glyph body. A naive `rgb()` regex is the other half of the same trap — anything built with `color-mix()` resolves to `color(srgb …)` and is skipped silently.
- The raster contrast method needs one more correction than it looks: a diff of the two renders finds every pixel the text touched, but most of those are the anti-aliased fringe, which is a blend of ink and ground by construction and scores near 1:1 whatever the design does. Solve for the coverage instead — the fill composited at the element's own opacity over the very ground under that pixel — and score only the pixels the glyph body owns. On the memory room that turns a meaningless 5.34% "below AA" into a real 0.14% (172 glyph pixels of 119,081 at 1280 real; 0.21% at 390; 0.04% at 1280 illustrative). It also found four real faults the stylesheet could not see, all of them low-opacity blue labels sitting on lit byte cells: the HEAD line at 3.73:1, the tensor name at 3.92:1, the window spec at 2.37:1 and the silent-register line at 1.51:1 median. Fixed by raising three opacities and by scrimming the wall behind a label in the screen's own ground.
- Instrument F draws no text over the picture without standing the picture back behind it. The wall dims to 55% under a stream and to 10% under a carrier or a label — and the dimming is a scrim painted in the screen's own background gradient rather than a per-cell alpha, which composites to exactly the same colour and keeps all 9,216 wall cells out of every re-render. The walls are memoised on the geometry alone: a keystroke redraws the streams, the carriers and the landing, and does not touch a single byte cell.
- Instrument F's lens whisper was removed in the arc 4 repaint. The drawing now says what the machine settled on at the landing instead, and instrument D still presents the same seven depths as a table, which is where they can actually be read. `src/lib/tensorTexture.js` and the `thumbnails` block of `fileFacts.json` are left in place but are no longer drawn by anything — dead weight to clear when something else touches that file.
